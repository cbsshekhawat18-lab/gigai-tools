## About
Gigai Tools is a privacy-first, browser-based productivity platform with 250+
free tools for images, PDFs, documents, video, audio, QR codes, SVG, SEO and
developers. Every tool runs entirely in the user's browser — files are processed
on-device and never uploaded. Free, no sign-up, no watermark. Gigai Tools is a
product of Gigai Kripa Services (https://gigaikripaservices.com).
